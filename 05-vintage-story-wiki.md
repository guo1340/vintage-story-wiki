# Vintage Story Wiki Expansion Project

## Project Objective

Transform the Vintage Story wiki from a database of game information into a practical survival handbook.

The goal is not merely to document game mechanics.

The goal is to answer questions such as:

- What should I prioritize first?
- What mistakes should I avoid?
- Is this worth doing?
- How do experienced players handle this situation?
- What is the most efficient progression path?

Google already understands item data.

This wiki should provide guidance and expertise.

---

# Content Philosophy

Vintage Story players rarely search for:

- item IDs
- raw statistics

Players typically search for:

- how to survive winter
- how to get food
- what to prioritize
- how farming works
- how metal progression works
- how to avoid dying

All generated content should focus on practical player outcomes.

# Tone Requirements

Write like:

- an experienced survivor
- a helpful veteran player

Do not write like:

- a textbook
- an encyclopedia
- a game manual

Good:

"Many new players underestimate how quickly food becomes a problem once winter arrives."

Bad:

"Food is used to restore hunger."

# Community Insight Sections

Every major guide should contain:

## Community Perspective

Summarize recurring player opinions.

Examples:

"Many experienced players recommend beginning farming immediately after securing a stable food source."

"Some players prefer a nomadic start, while others argue that permanent settlements provide a smoother progression path."

Do not quote directly.

Summarize trends.
