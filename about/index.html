About Vintage Story Wiki

Vintage Story Wiki is an unofficial fan-made survival handbook for players who want practical guidance without clutter. It focuses on early survival, farming, smithing, prospecting, world generation, creatures, building and mods.

This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Anego Studios or the Vintage Story team.

Editorial approach

Pages are written as field notes: direct, practical and focused on what decisions help a player survive longer. Exact values can change between versions, so mechanics-heavy pages include source and update notes.

Corrections

Use the contact page to report outdated mechanics, missing pages, broken links or source concerns.
